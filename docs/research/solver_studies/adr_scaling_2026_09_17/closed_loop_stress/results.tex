\paragraph{Completed campaign and reporting contract.}
The source records are the frozen \texttt{manifest.json} and per-job JSON
files under \path{run_outputs/solver_studies/}\allowbreak
\path{adr_closed_loop_stress_strong_numba_h150k/}.
This campaign used $p_{\rm FE}=6$ on $98\,699$ and $148\,848$ triangles
(targets 100k and 150k), giving $1\,021\,293$ and $1\,547\,455$ free trace
degrees of freedom. Each case/mesh shared one immutable matrix
and RHS across ten candidates. The \texttt{strong} preset used
ASM+PP(96), BJ+PP(96), GMRES/FGMRES restart 150, AMGX AMG W-cycles with
two pre/post sweeps, and two direct-DILU inner iterations with relaxation
0.7. Both $p$MG--AMG policies used Chebyshev order eight, three
$p$-level pre/post sweeps and three coarse-AMG pre/post sweeps with W-cycles.
The outer-iteration cap was $2\,000$, with internal tolerance $10^{-11}$
and acceptance based on the independently checked physical relative
residual $r_{\rm phys}=\|b-Ax\|_2/\|b\|_2\leq10^{-10}$.
One excluded warmup setup preceded three measured setups, each with two
zero-guess solves, provided the warmup passed. Separate profiles were
disabled. These data are not pooled with the earlier 50k/100k,
PP(48), restart-75 attempts.

Both meshes passed the implemented neck-size screen (minimum
gap/element-diameter ratios 8.947 and 8.683, against threshold six).
The requested \texttt{--neck-elements 12} controls mesh sizing, not a
verified twelve-element resolution guarantee. The original GPU runner
recorded no CPU direct reference at these sizes because both exceed its
$100\,000$-DOF reference limit. The confirmed LU baseline below now covers
all six saved trace systems. Screen passage and linear-solver
convergence are not $h/p$/quadrature accuracy certificates.

\paragraph{Convergence across all 60 comparisons.}
All 60 jobs completed: eight passed the numerical acceptance test and
52 ended in numerical failure. By case, the pass counts were
trapping $0/20$, crossing $0/20$, and orthogonal $8/20$.
Every AMGX candidate failed ($0/36$); all eight passes came from ASM+PP,
BJ+PP and the two $p$MG--AMG policies on the orthogonal control.
Table~\ref{tab:adr-closed-loop-residuals} retains every final residual,
including divergence; completion of the campaign is not convergence of
its failed systems.

\paragraph{Timings and field errors for converged solves.}
Table~\ref{tab:adr-closed-loop-timings} contains the eight passing GPU
configurations and the LU baseline on all six systems. GPU setup and
fresh setup-plus-first-solve times are medians
over three measured setups; reused time is the mean of the second solve
from each setup. Only preconditioner setup and linear solves are timed;
excluded warmups do not enter these statistics.
ASM+PP is fastest among tested GPU methods on both orthogonal meshes, with reused times 5.20 and
12.08\,s versus 8.30 and 16.17\,s for BJ+PP. The $p$MG--AMG robust policy
needs fewer iterations than standard (900 versus 1,350), but costs more
time. Outer iteration counts alone are not comparable work measures
between polynomial and multigrid preconditioners.
All four passing methods agree to the displayed precision on the primal
$L^2$ errors: $6.813\times10^{-7}$ at 100k and $3.613\times10^{-7}$ at
150k. This is an observed two-mesh decrease, not an established
asymptotic convergence rate; the later direct campaign validated the
trace solve, not these reconstructed primal errors.

\paragraph{Unconverged stress cases.}
ASM+PP gives the smallest final residual on each trapping/crossing system,
but still misses the target by factors from $3.84\times10^3$ to
$9.01\times10^4$. Its primal $L^2$ errors are 0.2190/0.3928 for trapping
and 0.01239/0.05034 for crossing at 100k/150k. These errors still mix
algebraic and discretization contributions: larger residuals and errors
on the finer mesh at a fixed iteration cap do not demonstrate worsening
discretization accuracy. Several AMGX PBICGSTAB runs diverge severely;
orthogonal MC-DILU reaches $2.604\times10^{16}$ at 100k and
$1.149\times10^{14}$ at 150k. Such failed-attempt costs are not
time-to-solution measurements.

\paragraph{Interpretation and remaining checks.}
The LU baseline solved all six saved stress systems
to the physical residual target (Table~\ref{tab:adr-closed-loop-timings}),
including trapping and crossing. Thus the GPU iteration failures do not
show that these discrete systems cannot be solved. They still require
conditioning, stabilization and discretization-accuracy checks before
assigning a cause. AMGX failure on the directly solvable orthogonal
control also motivates a configuration, hierarchy and operator-transfer
audit; it is not by itself proof of a software defect. ASM+PP is the
fastest passing tested GPU method on the orthogonal control and reduces
the residual most on trapping/crossing, but it did not solve those harder
systems to the required tolerance. Subsequent solver or stabilization
changes must be reported separately from this frozen GPU campaign.
