7. Preservation of equilibria in general geometry

   An unperturbed equilibrium provides a reference for separating numerical drift from the response to an imposed
diocotron perturbation. On a disk, radial symmetry supplies such states directly: a radial density generates a radial
potential, and the electric drift follows circles. For a noncircular wall, a prescribed radial density generally loses this
property. We instead construct localized states satisfying 𝜌★ = 𝐹 (𝜙★) and the self-consistent Poisson equation (6).
Their density is constant along the potential contours, so transport leaves them stationary in the continuous model.
We present four degree-six source equilibria and define the diagnostics for their subsequent transfer to HDG and
unperturbed evolution. HDG preservation tests of this family remain to be performed.

8. A diocotron benchmark in general geometry
    We are not aware of a published diocotron or Euler benchmark on domains with re-entrant corners. In this section
we propose one, built on the equilibria of Section 7. The design follows three rules: the perturbation and the observable
are defined from published data only; the reported growth rate is independent of the perturbation shape; and the
reference value comes with an uncertainty obtained from several resolutions, time integrators and an independent
CG-SUPG code.

8.1. Perturbation
     Let 𝑥 𝑐 be the maximum point of 𝜙★ (published), 𝜃 𝑐 the polar angle about 𝑥 𝑐 , and 𝑤 a smooth radial bump about
𝑥 𝑐 that vanishes near 𝑥 𝑐 and away from the band. We perturb the equilibrium by an infinitesimal area-preserving
displacement generated by

This perturbation has exactly zero mass and leaves all Casimirs unchanged to first order. On the disk it reduces
