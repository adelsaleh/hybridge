Definition: w(x) = min_{y on inner boundary} |x-y|, x on outer boundary.
Edges: outer phi=m-delta/2, inner phi=m+delta/2, both approximately rho=1/2.
Figure selection: run_outputs/equilibrium_dt080_20261016/article_selection.json.
Figure number verified from main.aux; four selected eps/delta=0.005 CG6 fields.

Method: 65,536 equally spaced outer-arclength samples per band, exact projection
onto inner polygon segments. A KD-tree candidate search is conservatively bounded.
At 128 random points and the two sampled extrema per geometry, exhaustive searches
over all inner segments agreed to within 4e-16 coordinate units.
Every saved outer vertex was checked additionally for extrema; these vertices
were not included in the uniformly weighted mean or standard deviation.

Observed width is nearly constant, with arclength standard deviation 0.98–2.30%
of the mean. Maximum local departures from the mean range from 3.27% to 7.86%.
The larger departures occur locally near strongly curved parts of the contours.

Numerical checks:
- Increasing outer samples from 16,384 to 65,536 changes mean width by <5.1e-9.
- The same increase changes sampled extrema by at most 2.12e-6.
- Distance is 1-Lipschitz: extrema over each saved outer polyline lie within
  L_outer/(2*65536) of uniform-sample extrema, a conservative bound.
- An independent check at 1,024 points per edge evaluated the full saved CG6
  polynomial and locally projected onto the threshold. Maximum corrections
  were <0.03% of mean width (sampled evidence, not a global bound).
- No equilibrium solve or time integration was performed.

Limitations: these checks verify distance evaluation and contour extraction.
They do not establish mesh convergence of the equilibria or exact constant width.
